01 / The Story
15YEARS OLD

Called early.
Still becoming.

Pastor Justin Wilson, A.A. is an ordained pastor, educator and youth advocate committed to developing the next generation.

A native of Brownsville, Tennessee, Justin was called to ministry early on, and began by preaching his first sermon at age 15. After graduating high school, he founded CrossBoys, a Christian hip-hop ministry that traveled nationally sharing the Gospel through music and testimony in schools, churches, correctional facilities and communities.

His journey has taken him through education, juvenile corrections, community service and pastoral leadership — experiences that continue to shape his approach to ministry, leadership and people.

“Helping the next generation recognize their identity, discover their purpose and become who God called them to be.”
