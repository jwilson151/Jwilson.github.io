Pastor Justin Wilson, A.A. is an ordained pastor, educator, youth advocate and speaker committed to developing the next generation.

A native of Brownsville, Tennessee, Justin began preaching at age 15. After graduating high school in 2009, he founded CrossBoys, a Christian hip-hop ministry that traveled nationally sharing the Gospel through music and testimony in schools, churches, correctional facilities and communities.

His journey has included education, juvenile corrections, community service, pastoral leadership and next-generation ministry. Today he serves as Associate Pastor and Director of Youth Ministry at Eagle Heights Cathedral.
